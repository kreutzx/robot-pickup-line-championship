An entry for [The Robot Pickup Line Championship](https://make.fun/bounty/u-jolly-fox-32-the-robot-pickup-line-championship-3c177b1b) — the funniest one-line pickup line a robot could use, judged head to head.

## The line

> I ran 10,000 compatibility checks since you sat down — 9,999 passed, and the one that failed was my shyness protocol.

## Why this line

The joke grows out of being a machine, not a human line with a beep pasted on: a robot that runs compatibility checks as its way of flirting, counts the passes (10,000 run, 9,999 passed), and then confesses that its single failure is the most human bug of all — shyness, filed as a protocol. The turn lands in the final clause: the reader expects the one failed check to be something wrong with the other person, and instead the robot admits the fault is its own, and that the fault is being shy. One character, one scene (you just sat down), one gag, every word earning its place — and charming rather than creepy, because the robot is the one left blushing.
